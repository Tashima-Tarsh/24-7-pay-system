Verification architecture

Seven controls.
One authoritative outcome.

These safeguards protect different parts of a payment. None of them independently proves that money entered a bank account.

Signed provider messages

Authenticate supported provider notifications before trusting their contents.

Replay protection

Reject duplicate event processing with durable identifiers and idempotent operations.

Request and token expiry

Bound payment sessions and authorization artifacts to valid time windows.

Merchant and payer attribution

Match the intended merchant, amount, currency and provider transaction reference.

Risk-based safeguards

Flag inconsistent requests or events without confusing risk signals with credit proof.

Reference integrity

Detect suspicious or repeated reference claims; a plausible UTR format is not evidence of settlement.

Authoritative confirmation

Accept a qualifying bank or PSP record before final payment success and ledger fulfillment.

Security model: signed messages, duplicate protection and device signals reduce specific risks, but payment success must ultimately be grounded in authorized provider or bank confirmation. Deployment-specific controls depend on enabled integrations.

Common questions

Answers before you integrate.

Does 24/7 Pay hold customer funds?

The direct merchant collection model routes supported payments to your configured receiving VPA. The payment platform is not intended to hold the merchant funds.

Does returning from Google Pay or PhonePe mean success?

No. UPI intent return and browser focus changes are not authoritative bank confirmation. Your backend must verify the actual payment using supported evidence.

Can a typed UTR release an order?

No. A UTR is a reference for matching, not standalone evidence that money was credited.

How do merchant callbacks work?

The backend can send signed events to the merchant endpoint. Merchants verify the signature and deduplicate event IDs; delivery attempts and errors appear in the workspace.

Where can I inspect the real dashboard?

Open the authenticated merchant workspace. Features are determined by account permissions and configured providers.
