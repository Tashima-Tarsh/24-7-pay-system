GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

Before you get started.

Can customers use their usual UPI apps?+

The checkout supports UPI payment instructions and app handoff on compatible devices. Available apps and success of the handoff depend on the device and UPI application.

When does a payment become confirmed?+

Not when a customer taps a button or enters a UTR. Confirmation requires an authorized, trusted payment source and the matching checks for the original order.

Can I connect this with my website or game?+

The backend includes authenticated payment APIs and signed callbacks. Your integration still needs merchant credentials, configuration and end-to-end testing.

Is every feature available immediately?+

Some features depend on your provider, receiving account and hosting configuration. Your workspace shows the information available for your account, while unconfigured services remain clearly identified.

Where do I see my payments and account settings?+

Use the merchant workspace to see payment history, operational updates and your permitted account settings. Platform operators have their own permission-based view.
